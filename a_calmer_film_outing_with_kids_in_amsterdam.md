# A calmer film outing with kids in Amsterdam

Family plans do not always need to involve a large playground, a packed attraction or a full day outside. Sometimes a film and a drink in a comfortable café are enough. Film & Café Het Ketelhuis offers that kind of outing, with a movie theater and café for families looking for a quieter plan in Amsterdam.

The cinema shows Dutch and international arthouse films. That gives parents another option when they want to do something cultural with their children while keeping the day simple. You can check the film listing first, then decide whether the café fits before or after the screening.
