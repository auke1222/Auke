# Planning an Airport Transfer with Children

Airport travel with children can make an ordinary journey feel more complicated than it needs to be. There are bags to organise, flight times to keep in mind, and the question of how everyone will get from the airport to the next stop.

For families travelling through the Netherlands, it helps to sort out the airport journey before travel day. A private airport transfer can provide a clear plan for getting to or from Schiphol Airport, especially when your trip also involves the Rotterdam area.

This is one of those details that is easy to leave until the last minute. Public transport may involve changes, and arranging a ride after landing can be tiring when children are exhausted too. Planning the airport portion early gives you one less decision to make during a busy travel day.
